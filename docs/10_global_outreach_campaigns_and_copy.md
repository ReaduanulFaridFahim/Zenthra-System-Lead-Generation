## 10.1 Universal Copywriting Standards
- **Strictly No Hollow Fluff:** Never use phrases such as "hope this email finds you well," "we are the leading AI agency," or "checking in."
- **Direct Pain-Signal Hook:** Every email opens with an auditable fact about the prospect's company.
- **Micro-Commitment CTA:** Never ask for a 30-minute sales demo on the first email. Ask for permission to share a 2-minute video teardown, an architectural blueprint, or a 10-minute discovery chat.
- **Mandatory Compliance Footer:** Include physical business address and a 1-click unsubscribe mechanism.
