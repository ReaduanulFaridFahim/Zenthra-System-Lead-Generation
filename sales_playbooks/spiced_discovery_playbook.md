# ZENTHRA SYSTEMS — SPICED DISCOVERY CALL PLAYBOOK
**Sales Rep Battle Guide | Executive Technology Sprints & Enterprise Engagements**

---

### Call Objectives (Target Duration: 25–30 Minutes)
1. Uncover the quantifiable financial cost of current manual processes or reporting latency.
2. Position Zenthra Systems as an agile, high-margin strike team (not a generic staff-aug vendor).
3. Gain agreement on the Critical Event driving timing.
4. Secure commitment to review a tailored Statement of Work (SOW) within 48 hours.

---

### Step 1: Rapport & Context Setting (Minutes 0–3)
> *"Hi {{first_name}}, thank you for taking the time today. The goal of this conversation is very straightforward: we want to understand how your team currently handles operational reporting and data workflows, and determine if our fixed-scope 5-Day Sprint makes sense for {{company}}. If there's a strong fit, we will prepare a technical scope for your team. If not, we'll share our operational architecture checklist and part ways as friends. Does that agenda sound fair to you?"*

---

### Step 2: Situation (Minutes 3–8)
* **Goal:** Map their current data architecture and operational tools.
* **Key Questions:**
  * *"Walk me through how your leadership team currently reviews gross margins, departmental KPIs, and performance numbers every Monday morning."*
  * *"What does your underlying data stack look like today? Which ERP or transactional database holds the source of truth?"*
  * *"How many different spreadsheets or exported CSVs are involved in compiling that weekly summary?"*

---

### Step 3: Pain (Minutes 8–14)
* **Goal:** Quantify the operational drag and human cost.
* **Key Questions:**
  * *"How many hours does your finance or operations team spend manually extracting, cleaning, and formatting that data before leadership can see it?"*
  * *"When numbers don't tie out between dispatch/sales and accounting, what is the process to reconcile them?"*
  * *"How does this latency impact your ability to identify underperforming accounts or cost overruns during the month?"*

---

### Step 4: Impact (Minutes 14–19)
* **Goal:** Translate pain into business risk and dollar terms.
* **Key Questions:**
  * *"If your senior finance/ops managers are spending 15 hours a week in Excel, that’s roughly 60 hours a month of high-value strategic capacity lost. Beyond the labor cost, what decisions are being delayed because you don't have real-time visibility?"*
  * *"If nothing changes over the next two quarters as your transaction volume grows, what happens to that reporting workload?"*

---

### Step 5: Critical Event (Minutes 19–23)
* **Goal:** Establish urgency and timeline anchors.
* **Key Questions:**
  * *"Is there an upcoming board meeting, fiscal year close, audit, or banking covenant review where having automated dashboards becomes non-negotiable?"*
  * *"What is the ideal date by which you need this operational visibility live?"*

---

### Step 6: Decision Process & Closing to SOW (Minutes 23–28)
* **Goal:** Uncover the buying committee and lock in the proposal review meeting.
* **Key Questions:**
  * *"Based on what we discussed, our 5-Day Executive KPI Sprint would connect directly to your ERP and eliminate that 15-hour weekly spreadsheet cycle in under two weeks."*
  * *"Aside from yourself, who else evaluates technical feasibility or signs off on project investments for your department?"*
  * *"I can have a complete Statement of Work with fixed pricing and architectural deliverables ready by tomorrow afternoon. Are you and {{other_stakeholder}} available for 20 minutes on Friday at 2:00 PM to review it?"*
